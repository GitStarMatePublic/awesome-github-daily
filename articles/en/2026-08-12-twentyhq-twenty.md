# Twenty: the open-source CRM built for the AI era — 54k stars

![twentyhq/twenty](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/live/twentyhq/twenty)

> TypeScript · NOASSERTION · 54,955 stars on GitHub

Twenty is an open-source alternative to Salesforce — a modern, customizable CRM designed from the ground up for the AI era. At 54k+ stars, it's the fastest-rising challenger to the bloated, expensive incumbents.

## The problem it solves

Legacy CRMs are expensive, clunky, and lock your most valuable asset — your customer data — inside a proprietary platform with per-seat pricing that punishes you for growing. Customizing them often means hiring a specialist.

## What I like

- Modern, fast UI that doesn't feel like enterprise software from 2010
- Open source and self-hostable — your customer data stays yours
- Built to be customizable and AI-native from the start

## One gripe

⚠️ It's younger than the incumbents, so the deep integration marketplace and enterprise features are still catching up.

## Verdict

If Salesforce's price and bloat make you wince, Twenty is the open, modern CRM finally worth switching your pipeline to.
