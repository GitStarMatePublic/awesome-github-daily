# CrewAI: orchestrate a crew of role-playing AI agents — 57k stars

![crewAIInc/crewAI](https://img.youtube.com/vi/-kSOTtYzgEw/hqdefault.jpg)

> Python · MIT · 57,088 stars on GitHub

CrewAI is a framework for orchestrating autonomous AI agents that take on roles and collaborate like a team — a researcher, a writer, a reviewer, working a task together. At 57k+ stars, it's one of the most popular ways to build agentic workflows.

## The problem it solves

Prompting one model to 'do everything' produces mushy, mediocre output. Real work benefits from division of labor and specialization — but coordinating multiple agents, their tools, and how they hand off work is a genuine engineering problem.

## What I like

- Intuitive 'crew of roles' mental model — easy to reason about
- Agents get their own goals, tools, and backstories for focused output
- Lean and standalone — not welded to one giant framework

## One gripe

⚠️ As with any multi-agent setup, costs and latency stack up, and more agents means more places for the plan to drift off course.

## Verdict

If the 'team of specialists' metaphor clicks for you, CrewAI is one of the most approachable on-ramps to real agentic workflows.
