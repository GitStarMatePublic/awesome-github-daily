# OpenCut: the open-source CapCut alternative that runs in your browser — 83k stars

![OpenCut-app/OpenCut](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/live/OpenCut-app/OpenCut)

> TypeScript · MIT · 83,270 stars on GitHub

OpenCut is a free, open-source video editor — a genuine CapCut alternative — created in mid-2025 and already past 83k stars. Timeline editing, no watermark, no subscription, and your footage never leaves your machine.

## The problem it solves

Popular editors like CapCut are slick but watch you: watermarks creep in, features move behind subscriptions, and your raw footage gets uploaded to their cloud with terms that change whenever they like.

## What I like

- Free and open source — no watermark, no subscription wall
- Privacy-first: your video stays local instead of uploading to a cloud
- Familiar timeline editing, approachable for non-pros

## One gripe

⚠️ It's young. It won't match a mature pro NLE like DaVinci or Premiere for deep features yet — it's evolving fast, but manage expectations.

## Verdict

If CapCut's watermarks and creeping paywalls have worn you down, OpenCut is the open, private editor to watch — and it's improving quickly.
