# PrivateGPT: ask questions of your documents with zero internet — 57k stars

![zylon-ai/private-gpt](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/live/zylon-ai/private-gpt)

> Python · Apache-2.0 · 57,445 stars on GitHub

PrivateGPT is a complete API layer for building private, offline AI apps over your own documents — no data ever leaves your machine. At 57k+ stars, it was one of the original 'chat with your files, 100% local' projects and remains a reference implementation.

## The problem it solves

The whole promise of AI over your documents collapses the moment 'your documents' get uploaded to a third-party API. For legal, medical, or just plain private data, that's a non-starter — but rolling your own local pipeline is intimidating.

## What I like

- 100% offline option — documents and queries never leave your box
- A clean API layer to build private AI apps on top of
- Battle-tested and widely referenced for local RAG patterns

## One gripe

⚠️ Local-only means local performance — without a GPU, responses over large document sets can be slow.

## Verdict

When 'private' is a hard requirement, not a nice-to-have, PrivateGPT is the proven starting point for fully offline document AI.
