# Appwrite: the open-source backend platform for web, mobile and AI — 56k stars

![appwrite/appwrite](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/55a81268-4ecc-46cd-bdf5-73f7e8662fee)

> TypeScript · BSD-3-Clause · 56,987 stars on GitHub

Appwrite is a complete open-source backend platform — auth, databases, storage, functions, messaging — for web, mobile, and AI apps. At 56k+ stars, it's a leading self-hostable alternative to proprietary backend clouds.

## The problem it solves

Every app re-implements the same backend chores: user auth, a database, file uploads, push notifications, serverless functions. Building them from scratch is repetitive; renting them from a closed platform means lock-in and surprise pricing.

## What I like

- A broad, coherent backend suite behind clean SDKs for every platform
- Self-hostable, so your users' data and your bill stay predictable
- Strong docs and DX — you're productive on day one

## One gripe

⚠️ Running the full self-hosted platform is more moving parts to operate than a single-file backend; scale it and you inherit real ops.

## Verdict

For a serious app that needs a full backend without vendor lock-in, Appwrite is one of the most complete open platforms available.
