# opencode: the open-source coding agent that isn't locked to one editor — 197k stars

![anomalyco/opencode](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/live/anomalyco/opencode)

> TypeScript · MIT · 197,581 stars on GitHub

opencode is a terminal-first, fully open-source coding agent, and it's rocketed past 197k stars. The pitch is simple: an agent that lives in your terminal, works with any model, and isn't tied to a single IDE or vendor.

## The problem it solves

Most AI coding agents want to own your whole environment — their editor, their model, their subscription. If you love your existing terminal workflow, that's a hard sell. And vendor lock-in on the thing that writes your code is a real risk.

## What I like

- Terminal-native — drops into the workflow you already have
- Model-agnostic: bring your own provider, no forced subscription
- Fully open source, so you can audit and extend the agent itself

## One gripe

⚠️ Terminal-first means no rich GUI hand-holding — if you want a polished point-and-click IDE experience, this isn't it (by design).

## Verdict

For developers who live in the terminal and refuse to be locked into one vendor's coding agent, opencode is the open answer.
