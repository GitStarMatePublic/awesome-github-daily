# RustDesk: the open-source TeamViewer you can fully self-host — 120k stars

![rustdesk/rustdesk](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/71636191/171661982-430285f0-2e12-4b1d-9957-4a58e375304d.png)

> Rust · AGPL-3.0 · 120,708 stars on GitHub

RustDesk is an open-source remote desktop built for self-hosting, and it's earned 120k+ stars. Same 'control my other machine from anywhere' magic as the commercial tools — but you can run your own relay server and own the whole thing.

## The problem it solves

Commercial remote-desktop tools are great until they flag your normal usage as 'commercial,' nag you, or hike the price. And routing your screen and keystrokes through someone else's servers is a real privacy question.

## What I like

- Self-hostable relay server — your sessions never touch a third party
- Cross-platform: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS
- Written in Rust, no account required to get started

## One gripe

⚠️ Self-hosting the relay for best performance is extra setup — the convenience of a hosted option comes with the usual trust trade-off.

## Verdict

If you've been nagged or price-gouged by commercial remote-desktop tools, RustDesk gives you the same power with full control over your data.
