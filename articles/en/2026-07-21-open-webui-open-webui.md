# Open WebUI: a self-hosted ChatGPT for your own models — 148k stars

![open-webui/open-webui](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/live/open-webui/open-webui)

> Python · NOASSERTION · 148,812 stars on GitHub

Open WebUI gives you a polished, ChatGPT-like interface that runs entirely on your infrastructure, talking to Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible API. 148k+ stars, and it's the front-end of choice for the self-hosted AI crowd.

## The problem it solves

Running models locally with Ollama is great — until you want a real chat UI with history, multiple users, document upload and RAG. The raw API isn't something you hand to your less-technical teammates.

## What I like

- Clean multi-user chat UI with history, roles and permissions
- Works with local (Ollama) and remote OpenAI-compatible models alike
- Built-in RAG: drop in documents and chat over them, no extra service

## One gripe

⚠️ Feature growth means the setup has more moving parts than 'just a chat box' — plan a little time for configuration to get everything wired.

## Verdict

If you want the ChatGPT experience without sending a byte to someone else's cloud, Open WebUI is the front-end to put on top of your models.
