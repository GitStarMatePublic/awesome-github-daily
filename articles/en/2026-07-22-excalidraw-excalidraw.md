# Excalidraw: the hand-drawn whiteboard that everyone secretly loves — 129k stars

![excalidraw/excalidraw](https://excalidraw.nyc3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/github/excalidraw_github_cover_2.png)

> TypeScript · MIT · 129,610 stars on GitHub

Excalidraw is a virtual whiteboard for sketching diagrams that look charmingly hand-drawn. At 129k+ stars, it's quietly become the default tool for quick architecture sketches, and it's fully open source and embeddable.

## The problem it solves

Heavyweight diagram tools make you fight menus and pixel-perfect alignment when all you wanted was to sketch a box-and-arrow idea in 30 seconds. The polish actively gets in the way of thinking.

## What I like

- The hand-drawn style signals 'this is a draft' — people give feedback freely
- Zero-friction: open the tab, start drawing, no account needed
- Open source and embeddable — drop the canvas into your own app

## One gripe

⚠️ It's deliberately not a precision CAD/flowchart tool — if you need exact, structured, data-bound diagrams, look elsewhere.

## Verdict

For thinking out loud in boxes and arrows, nothing beats Excalidraw's friction-free, deliberately-rough charm.
