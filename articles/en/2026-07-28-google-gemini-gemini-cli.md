# Gemini CLI: Google's open-source coding agent, right in your terminal — 106k stars

![google-gemini/gemini-cli](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/live/google-gemini/gemini-cli)

> TypeScript · Apache-2.0 · 106,524 stars on GitHub

Gemini CLI is Google's open-source AI agent that brings Gemini straight into your terminal — code, debug, and automate without leaving the shell. It's already at 106k+ stars, a sign of how fast the terminal-agent space is heating up.

## The problem it solves

Switching between your editor, a browser chat window, and the terminal to get AI help is death by context-switch. Copy-pasting code back and forth loses the very context that makes the answer good.

## What I like

- Gemini's large context window lands right where you work — the shell
- Open source and scriptable, so it slots into automation and CI
- Backed by Google, with a generous free tier to start

## One gripe

⚠️ It's tied to Gemini models — great if that's your stack, less flexible if you're committed to another provider.

## Verdict

If you live in the terminal and want a capable, free-to-start coding agent from a major lab, Gemini CLI is an easy one to keep in your toolbox.
