# AnythingLLM: an all-in-one private ChatGPT over your own documents — 64k stars

![Mintplex-Labs/anything-llm](https://github.com/Mintplex-Labs/anything-llm/blob/master/images/wordmark.png?raw=true)

> JavaScript · MIT · 64,720 stars on GitHub

AnythingLLM is a full-stack, private AI application: chat with any LLM over your own documents, with built-in RAG, agents, and no data leaving your control. At 64k+ stars, it's the 'own your intelligence' answer to renting a chatbot.

## The problem it solves

Uploading your company's confidential docs into someone else's chatbot is a compliance nightmare, but building your own RAG stack from scratch is weeks of work. Most teams get stuck between 'unsafe but easy' and 'safe but never ships.'

## What I like

- Chat over your documents with RAG, fully self-hostable
- Works with local or commercial models — your choice of provider
- Multi-user workspaces, so a whole team can use it privately

## One gripe

⚠️ All-in-one means opinionated defaults; deeply custom RAG pipelines may still want a more composable, code-first stack.

## Verdict

For a team that wants a private ChatGPT-over-our-docs without building the plumbing, AnythingLLM is the fastest safe path there.
