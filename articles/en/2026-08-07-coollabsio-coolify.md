# Coolify: self-host your apps like it's Vercel, on your own server — 60k stars

![coollabsio/coolify](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/live/coollabsio/coolify)

> PHP · Apache-2.0 · 60,600 stars on GitHub

Coolify is an open-source, self-hostable PaaS — a Vercel/Heroku/Netlify alternative you run on your own hardware. At 60k+ stars, it's the way to get push-to-deploy convenience without the per-seat cloud bill.

## The problem it solves

Managed platforms are magic until the bill arrives. Deploy a few apps, add a database, some background workers, and suddenly you're paying startup-sized money to host a side project — for compute a cheap VPS could handle.

## What I like

- Push-to-deploy DX on your own server or any VPS
- Manages apps, databases, and services with automatic HTTPS
- One flat cost (your server) instead of scaling per-resource cloud fees

## One gripe

⚠️ You become the ops team — self-hosting means you own the uptime, backups, and updates that a managed platform handled for you.

## Verdict

If cloud platform bills have outgrown your side projects, Coolify hands you the same deploy experience for the price of a VPS.
