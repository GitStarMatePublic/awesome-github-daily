# Immich: self-hosted Google Photos that's finally good enough to switch — 110k stars

![immich-app/immich](https://opengraph.githubassets.com/live/immich-app/immich)

> TypeScript · AGPL-3.0 · 110,544 stars on GitHub

Immich is a high-performance, self-hosted photo and video manager — and at 110k+ stars, it's become the project people actually leave Google Photos for. Auto-backup from your phone, face recognition, timeline, the works.

## The problem it solves

Cloud photo services are convenient until the free tier shrinks, the price climbs, and you realize your entire family's memories live on someone else's server under someone else's terms. Exporting later is a nightmare.

## What I like

- Automatic phone backup that genuinely rivals the Google Photos flow
- ML features — face recognition, search, smart albums — all local
- Fast, modern UI that doesn't feel like a hobby project

## One gripe

⚠️ It's still pre-1.0 and moves fast — you're responsible for backups, and breaking changes do happen. Don't run it as your only copy.

## Verdict

Immich is the first self-hosted photo app polished enough to actually replace Google Photos — just keep real backups while it matures.
